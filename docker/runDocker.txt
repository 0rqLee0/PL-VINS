  euroc/mh01_tum_noloop.txt       (无回环轨迹，TUM格式)
  euroc/mh01_evo_noloop.txt       (无回环轨迹，EVO格式)
  euroc/mh01_tum_loop.txt         (回环优化轨迹，TUM格式)
  euroc/mh01_evo_loop.txt         (回环优化轨迹，EVO格式)
  euroc/mh01_tum_globalloop.txt   (全局回环优化轨迹，TUM格式)
  euroc/mh01_evo_globalloop.txt   (全局回环优化轨迹，EVO格式)

===== TUM-VI 数据集 =====

终端1 - 运行SLAM：
  cd docker/
  ./run_docker.sh

  # 容器内运行（通过 sequence_name 指定输出文件名）
  roslaunch plvins_estimator tumvi_no_rviz.launch sequence_name:=room1 & rosbag play /datasets/TUM/room1.bag
  roslaunch plvins_estimator tumvi_no_rviz.launch sequence_name:=room2 & rosbag play /datasets/TUM/room2.bag
  roslaunch plvins_estimator tumvi_no_rviz.launch sequence_name:=room3 & rosbag play /datasets/TUM/room3.bag
  roslaunch plvins_estimator tumvi_no_rviz.launch sequence_name:=room4 & rosbag play /datasets/TUM/room4.bag
  roslaunch plvins_estimator tumvi_no_rviz.launch sequence_name:=room5 & rosbag play /datasets/TUM/room5.bag
  roslaunch plvins_estimator tumvi_no_rviz.launch sequence_name:=room6 & rosbag play /datasets/TUM/room6.bag

终端2 - 运行RViz（宿主机）：
  cd docker/
  ./host_rviz.sh

结果输出在 /home/lee/Results/line/PLVINS/tumvi/ 下

注意事项：
  1. 数据集目录默认挂载到容器内 /datasets，请确保 /home/lee/Documents/Datasets_VSLAM 下有对应数据集
  2. 如需修改数据集或结果路径，编辑 docker-compose.yml 中的 volumes
  3. host_rviz.sh 需要宿主机已安装 nvidia-docker（--gpus all）并允许 X11 转发
  4. 每次运行新序列前无需手动清空结果文件，程序启动时会自动清空对应序列的输出文件
